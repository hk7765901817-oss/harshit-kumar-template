Immersive Web Experience

Meridian

Client

Meridian Technologies

Year

2026

Services

WebGL, UI/UX, Motion

The Challenge

Meridian Technologies needed a digital presence that matched their ambition to redefine spatial computing. Their existing website failed to communicate the transformative nature of their products, feeling static and conventional in a market demanding innovation.

The Solution

We created an immersive WebGL experience that places users inside Meridian's spatial computing environment from the moment they land on the page. Custom shaders simulate light refraction through holographic interfaces, while scroll-driven animations guide users through product features with cinematic pacing.

The interface employs a glass-morphism design language with real-time environmental reflections, creating a sense of depth and tactility. Every interaction was choreographed to feel like manipulating physical objects in space.

The Process

Our team spent three weeks in deep research, analyzing spatial computing interfaces and light physics. We developed a custom shader library for real-time glass refraction and built a bespoke scroll engine that maps physical scroll input to 3D camera movements.

The design system was built around the concept of "luminous interfaces"—elements that appear to emit light rather than reflect it. This required careful color science, HDR rendering techniques, and meticulous attention to contrast ratios.
